How our plans work

  • Joining a plan is free. There's no subscription fee and nothing is charged for choosing one — you only ever pay for the cleanings you book.
  • Your first cleaning on a plan is full price. The plan starts with it, so there's no discount to apply yet.
  • The discount lands on your second cleaning in a row, and on every one after it, as long as you book again within the plan's window.
  • Miss the window and the plan simply lapses. Nothing is charged and nothing is owed — your next cleaning starts a fresh plan at full price.
  • You can change or drop your plan at any time, here or while you're booking.
