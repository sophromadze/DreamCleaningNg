What Is Included in Our NYC Airbnb Turnover Cleaning Checklist

1. Kitchen Reset and Guest Prep

Our NYC turnover cleaning team prepares your kitchen so it looks brand new, immaculate, and welcoming for incoming guests.

  • Surfaces & Counters: Full scrubbing and sanitizing of countertops, stovetops, and tiled backsplashes.
  • Appliance Polishing: Cleaning and polishing all major kitchen appliance exteriors (refrigerator, stove, oven, dishwasher).
  • Microwave Interior: Deep cleaning the inside of the microwave to eliminate food splatters and odors.
  • Sink Disinfection: Scrubbing, disinfecting, and drying the sink while polishing chrome fixtures.
  • Fridge & Oven Inspection: Checking inside the refrigerator and oven for left-behind food or trash and doing a quick spot clean.
  • Cabinets & Handles: Wiping down cabinet exteriors, edges, and high-touch handles to remove grease and fingerprints.
  • Floors & Trash: Vacuuming and damp-mopping floors; disposing of guest trash and sanitizing the bins.

2. Bathroom Sanitization and Staging

We restore your rental bathroom with industrial-grade scrubbing and deep disinfection, ensuring a high-end hotel feel.

  • Toilet Deep Clean: Complete sanitization of the toilet bowl, rim, base, and the hard-to-reach area behind the tank.
  • Vanity & Sink: Scrubbing sinks, faucets, and vanity surfaces until completely spotless and streak-free.
  • Mirrors & Chrome: Polishing mirrors and fixtures to a crystal-clear, bright shine.
  • Shower & Tub Scrubbing: Detailed scrubbing of shower glass doors, tracks, and bathtubs to eliminate soap scum and water spots.
  • Floors & Presentation: Washing and sanitizing bathroom floors; staging fresh towels and restocking essential toiletries (toilet paper, hand soap).

3. Living Areas and Bedrooms

We bring freshness back to your NYC living spaces, focusing heavily on aesthetic presentation and high-touch sanitation.

  • Hotel-Style Bed Making: Stripping old sheets, changing bed linens, and making beds to hotel standards (when clean linens are provided on-site).
  • Detailed Dusting: Dusting all accessible surfaces, coffee tables, nightstands, and decorative shelving.
  • Glass & Mirrors: Cleaning interior glass surfaces, windowsills, and mirrors.
  • High-Touch Disinfection: Sanitizing door frames, door handles, light switches, and TV remote controls.
  • Floor Care: Thoroughly vacuuming carpets, area rugs, and mopping hardwood, laminate, or tile floors.
  • Staging & Decor: Light straightening of decor, fluffing couch cushions, and neatly arranging pillows for a perfect visual first impression.
  • Lost & Found Walkthrough: A final check for any items left behind by previous guests so you can notify them and avoid bad reviews.
