Important Post Construction Cleaning Information:

  • We assume the commercial space is cleared and all construction work is finished
  • No trades or workers should be on-site during our cleaning service
  • Construction dust settles everywhere — walls, ceilings, ducts, small gaps and notches — so some residual dust may continue to appear for several weeks after cleaning
  • Notify us in advance about paint, putty, adhesive, or stickers requiring removal
  • We provide all cleaning supplies except a vacuum cleaner and stepladder

Our Comprehensive Commercial Post Construction Cleaning Includes:

All Areas Throughout Your Commercial Space:

  • Thorough dusting and vacuuming of floors, walls, and ceilings
  • Deep mopping and machine-friendly cleaning of all hard floors (please provide a mop if preferred)
  • Detailed cleaning inside all closets, storage rooms, cabinets, and drawers
  • Interior glass, storefront, and partition washing including frames and sills (notify us for oversized glazing)
  • Complete baseboard cleaning and dust removal
  • Construction debris and trash removal (please specify items to keep)
  • Removal of construction dust from small gaps, notches, ducts, and hard-to-reach areas

  • Thorough sanitization of countertops, prep surfaces, and stovetops
