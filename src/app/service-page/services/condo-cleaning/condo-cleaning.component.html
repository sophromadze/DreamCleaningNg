Choose Your Service Level: Our Core Cleaning Categories

Every home is in a different state of care. To ensure you get the exact service you need, we categorize our condo cleaning into distinct operational tiers:

Regular Condo Clean (Maintenance)

Designed for normally maintained condos that just need a surface-level polish, dusting, and vacuum/mop on a recurring weekly or bi-weekly basis.

Deep Condo Clean (Lived-in Reset)

Recommended for your first visit or as a seasonal refresh. Includes everything in Regular, plus hand-scrubbing baseboards, detailed tile grout cleaning, and dusting ceiling fans and blinds.

Move-In / Move-Out Clean (Empty Unit)

Preparing to vacate or move into a new condo? This package is for 100% empty units and includes deep cleaning inside and outside all kitchen cabinets, closets, and major appliances (fridge/oven).

Heavy Condition Reset (Neglected Spaces)

If a condo hasn't been cleaned in several months and features thick dust buildup, sticky kitchen cabinets, and heavy limescale in the showers, we deploy an intensive, heavy-duty crew to restore order.

Filthy Cleanup (Emergency Remediation)

If a unit has faced absolute neglect, severe hoarding issues, or pet/human bio-hazard waste, we transition to our specialized hourly team equipped with protective gear and industrial sanitizers.

Post-Construction Condo Clean

Newly renovated or built high-rise unit? We use specialized techniques to neutralize fine white drywall dust, scrape paint drops from trim, and clean fine dust out of cabinet tracks and hinges.

What Is Included in Our NYC Condo Cleaning Checklist

1. Living Areas and Bedrooms

We refresh your main relaxation spaces, meticulously dusting around your personal furniture, electronics, and decor.

  • Surface Dusting: Detailed hand-dusting of all accessible tables, nightstands, desks, and open shelving.
  • Floor Care: Thorough vacuuming of area rugs, carpets, and under accessible furniture, followed by a specialized damp-mop for hardwood, laminate, or tile floors.
  • Glass & Mirrors: Cleaning and polishing interior glass surfaces, framed mirrors, and glass tabletops.
  • High-Touch Wiping: Wiping down light switches, door handles, and visible trim to remove smudges.
  • Bed Making: Tidying up the bedroom, fluffing pillows, straightening blankets, and making the bed (or changing linens if a fresh set is left out).
  • Trim & Sills: Wiping down visible baseboards and accessible windowsills (Deep & Heavy Condition tiers).

2. Kitchen Deep-Wipe

We clean and sanitize your condo's kitchen, focusing on removing daily cooking oils and maintaining a polished presentation.

  • Countertop Care: Sanitizing premium quartz, granite, or marble countertops and wiping down tile or glass backsplashes.
  • Appliance Exteriors: Wiping down and polishing the outer surfaces of your refrigerator, stove, oven, and dishwasher to a streak-free shine.
  • Microwave Clean: Wiping down the microwave inside and out to remove food splatters.
  • Sink & Faucets: Scrubbing and disinfecting the kitchen sink basin and polishing the chrome or matte fixtures.
  • Cabinet Faces: Wiping down cabinet doors, handles, and lower trim to remove grease and fingerprints (interior included in Move-In/Post-Construction only).
  • Floors & Waste: Emptying the kitchen trash, replacing the bag, and executing a full vacuum and mopping cycle.

3. Bathroom Sanitization

We deliver deep disinfection to your private baths, carefully treating tile, porcelain, and glass fixtures.

  • Toilet Sanitization: Complete chemical scrubbing and disinfection of the toilet bowl, rim, seat, and base.
  • Vanity & Sink: Scrubbing the sink basin, wiping down vanity countertops, and polishing the mirror.
  • Shower & Tub Scrubbing: Washing shower glass doors, tracks, tile walls, and bathtubs to eliminate soap scum and water spots.
  • Grout & Scale Treatment: Detailed grout line scrubbing and limescale removal from plumbing fixtures (Deep, Heavy, and Move-In tiers).
  • Floors & Finishing: Emptying small waste bins, sweeping, and sanitizing the bathroom floor.
