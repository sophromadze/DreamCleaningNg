Choose Your Service Level: Our Core Cleaning Categories

Every home requires a different level of care depending on your daily routine. We categorize our house cleaning into distinct operational tiers to give you exactly what your property needs:

Regular House Clean (Maintenance)

Designed for active family homes that need a consistent surface-level polish, dusting, and full vacuum/mop on a recurring weekly or bi-weekly schedule to keep up with daily life.

Deep House Clean (Lived-in Reset)

Perfect as a seasonal refresh or first-time visit. Includes everything in Regular, plus hand-scrubbing baseboards, detailed tile grout cleaning in large bathrooms, and dusting ceiling fans, blinds, and high window trim.

Move-In / Move-Out Clean (Empty Home)

Selling your house, passing a landlord inspection, or moving into a new property? This package is for 100% empty houses and covers deep cleaning inside and outside all kitchen cabinets, bedroom closets, and major kitchen appliances (fridge/oven).

Heavy Condition Reset (Neglected Houses)

If a house has been left unmaintained for several months, resulting in thick dust, sticky kitchen surfaces, and heavy scale buildup in showers, our intensive heavy-duty crew will restore order.

Filthy Cleanup (Emergency Remediation)

If a property has faced severe neglect, extreme hoarding situations, or pet/human bio-hazard waste, we deploy our specialized hourly team equipped with protective gear and industrial-strength sanitizers.

Post-Construction House Clean

Just finished a major home extension, kitchen remodel, or full renovation? We use a multi-stage process to eliminate fine white drywall dust, scrape paint splatters off trim, and clean fine dust out of heating vents, tracks, and hinges.

What Is Included in Our NYC House Cleaning Checklist

1. Bedrooms, Living Rooms & High-Traffic Areas

We refresh your main family spaces and bedrooms, meticulously removing dust from large layouts and heavy-use zones.

  • Detailed Dusting: Hand-dusting all accessible tables, nightstands, desks, entertainment centers, and open shelving.
  • Floor Care: Deep vacuuming of large area rugs, carpets, staircases, and hallways, followed by a specialized damp-mop for hardwood, tile, or laminate floors.
  • Glass & Mirrors: Cleaning and polishing interior glass partitions, framed mirrors, and glass tabletops.
  • High-Touch Wiping: Disinfecting high-use surfaces like light switches, door handles, and banisters.
  • Bed Making: Tidying up bedrooms, fluffing pillows, straightening blankets, and making beds (or changing linens if fresh sheets are left out).
  • Trim & Detailed Woodwork: Wiping down visible baseboards, door frames, and accessible windowsills (Deep & Heavy Condition tiers).

2. Kitchen Deep-Wipe

We clean and sanitize your home's kitchen, focusing on removing built-up cooking oils, grease, and maintaining a spotless presentation.

  • Countertop Care: Sanitizing large island counters, quartz, granite, or marble surfaces and wiping down tiled backsplashes.
  • Appliance Exteriors: Wiping down and polishing the outer surfaces of your refrigerator, stove, oven, and dishwasher to a streak-free shine.
  • Microwave Clean: Wiping down the microwave inside and out to remove food splatters.
  • Sink & Faucets: Scrubbing and disinfecting large kitchen sinks, basins, and polishing the fixtures.
  • Cabinet Faces: Wiping down cabinet doors, handles, and lower trim to remove grease and fingerprints (interior included in Move-In/Post-Construction only).
  • Floors & Waste: Emptying large kitchen trash bins, replacing the liners, and executing a full vacuum and mopping cycle.

3. Bathroom Sanitization

We deliver deep disinfection to your family baths, carefully treating tile, porcelain, and glass fixtures.

  • Toilet Sanitization: Complete chemical scrubbing and disinfection of the toilet bowl, rim, seat, and base.
  • Vanity & Sink: Scrubbing the sink basin, wiping down vanity countertops, and polishing the mirror.
  • Shower & Tub Scrubbing: Washing shower glass doors, tracks, tile walls, and large bathtubs to eliminate soap scum and water spots.
  • Grout & Scale Treatment: Detailed grout line scrubbing and limescale removal from plumbing fixtures (Deep, Heavy, and Move-In tiers).
  • Floors & Finishing: Emptying small waste bins, sweeping, and sanitizing the bathroom floor.
