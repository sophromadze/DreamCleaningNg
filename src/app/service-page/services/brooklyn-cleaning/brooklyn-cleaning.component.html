Cleaning Service in Every Brooklyn Neighborhood

From pre-war brownstones in Park Slope to renovated lofts in DUMBO, every Brooklyn neighborhood has its own kind of home — and its own kind of mess. Our cleaners know the layout of railroad apartments, the quirks of fourth-floor walk-ups, and the parking realities of Bay Ridge on a Saturday morning. Below is what we run into most often in each of the areas we cover.

Park Slope

Limestones and brownstones along 7th and 5th Avenue, plus the family co-ops near Prospect Park. We get a lot of recurring biweekly cleanings here for families with young kids and pets — bathrooms, kitchens, and the original wood floors get the most attention. ZIP codes 11215 and 11217.

Williamsburg & Greenpoint

Industrial-style lofts off Bedford Avenue, new-construction high-rises near the waterfront, and a steady stream of short-term rental turnovers. Airbnb cleanings, deep cleanings between roommates, and post-party resets are our top requests here. ZIP codes 11211, 11206, 11222.

DUMBO & Brooklyn Heights

High-ceilinged converted warehouses in DUMBO and historic townhouses on Pierrepont and Hicks. Floors are usually concrete or original hardwood and the windows are tall — we plan extra time for both. Move in/out cleanings here are common because of the rental turnover. ZIP codes 11201, 11251.

Bay Ridge & Bensonhurst

Single-family homes, semi-attached row houses, and family-owned co-ops along 4th and 86th. Standard recurring cleanings dominate here, often weekly, and many clients add laundry or oven cleaning. We're already in the area daily. ZIP codes 11209, 11214, 11220, 11228.

Bushwick & Bedford-Stuyvesant

Renovated three-flats, basement apartments, and shared rentals where the kitchen and bathroom carry most of the cleaning load. We do a lot of one-time deep cleanings before lease renewals here, plus heavy condition restorations for places that haven't been cleaned in months. ZIP codes 11221, 11237, 11216, 11238.

Sunset Park & Flatbush

Pre-war apartment buildings, converted multi-families, and a mix of long-term tenants and new arrivals. Recurring residential cleaning and post-construction cleanups after small-scale renovations are the most common bookings. ZIP codes 11220, 11232, 11226, 11210.

Deep Cleaning & Post-Construction Cleaning Brooklyn

A deep clean covers what the recurring routine skips — baseboards, behind appliances, inside the oven and fridge, light fixtures, vents, and the slow-buildup areas a weekly visit doesn't have time for.
