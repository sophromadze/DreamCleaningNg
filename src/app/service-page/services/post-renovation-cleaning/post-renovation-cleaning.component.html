Important Post Renovation Cleaning Information:

  • All renovation work should be finished and contractors off-site before we arrive
  • Fine renovation dust settles everywhere — walls, ceilings, trim, and small gaps — so a little residual dust may continue to surface for a few weeks afterward
  • Tell us in advance about paint drips, adhesive, grout haze, or putty marks that need special attention
  • We work best in rooms that are cleared of tools, materials, and packaging (let us know which items to keep)
  • We provide all cleaning supplies except a vacuum cleaner and stepladder

Our Comprehensive Post Renovation Cleaning Includes:

Every Room in Your Renovated Home:

  • Thorough dusting and vacuuming of floors, walls, and ceilings to lift settled renovation dust
  • Damp-mopping of all hard floors to remove gritty residue (please provide a mop if you prefer one)
  • Detailed cleaning inside refreshed closets, cabinets, and drawers
  • Interior window washing including frames, sills, and glass to clear renovation haze
  • Complete baseboard, trim, and door-frame wipe-down
  • Light debris and packaging removal (please specify what to keep)
  • Dust removal from small gaps, notches, outlets, and hard-to-reach corners

Remodeled Kitchen Detailing:

  • Cleaning new cabinets inside and out to remove sawdust and fingerprints
  • Sanitizing countertops, stovetops, and backsplashes after installation
  • Wiping and polishing new appliance exteriors and fixtures
  • Removing dust and adhesive residue from newly fitted surfaces
  • Floor detailing to clear construction grit from grout lines and edges

Renovated Bathroom Refresh:

  • Scrubbing new tile, tubs, toilets, and sinks to remove installation residue
  • Complete sanitization and disinfection of all bathroom surfaces
  • Polishing new faucets, mirrors, and fixtures to a clear shine
  • Tile and grout cleaning to lift grout haze and dust

Renovation Detail Work:

  • Removing paint specks, adhesive marks, and renovation-specific spots from surfaces
  • Sanitizing high-touch areas like door handles, railings, and light switches
  • Dust removal from vents, fixtures, and freshly painted trim
  • A custom cleaning plan shaped around the rooms you renovated
