Our Story

Dream Cleaning wasn't created because cleaning services didn't exist — it was created because customers were so often missing what matters most: quality, accountability, and a calm, worry-free experience.

Before we started the company, we were customers ourselves. For years we watched how different companies worked, studied the problems that kept coming up, and saw the difficulties people faced when they trusted a professional with their home, office, or other space. A few problems showed up again and again:

  • Inconsistent quality of service
  • The wrong type of service chosen for the job
  • Mismatched expectations between the customer and the cleaner
  • More focus on hours worked than on the actual result
  • Too little communication and oversight while the work was happening

Too often, customers had no clear idea of what to expect, while companies never fully understood what actually mattered to the client. That gap is what creates uncertainty, disappointment, and distrust — so we decided to do things differently.

How We Work

For us, every space and every client is individual. That's why our process starts not with a booking, but with a careful understanding of your needs.

1

The right service, fast answers

Our specialists help you choose the service that truly fits your space — and they're ready to respond quickly, with no long waits on hold.

2

A shared checklist

Once your booking is confirmed, you receive a detailed checklist of exactly what will be done. The same checklist goes to our specialist and to the cleaner — so every side has the same complete information.

3

Quality control on your side

Throughout the job, our team monitors the quality of the work, protects your interests, and makes sure everything is done according to the agreed plan.

4

Updates whenever you want them

You can reach our specialist at any time for an update on how the work is progressing — even if you're not there in person.

5

Live progress photos

We capture before-and-after and progress photos during the job, so we can continuously verify the result and confirm everything meets your requirements.

6

A heads-up before we finish

About 30 minutes before completion, our representative reaches out so you can review the result and have any final details taken care of.

This approach minimizes the chance of missing any detail that matters to you.
